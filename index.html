NetraVaniनेत्रवाणी · आँखों से बात

Eye-gaze keyboard for ICU patients who cannot speak or use their hands. The patient looks at a key and chooses it with a deliberate eye closure. The phone speaks the message aloud.

  1. Fix the phone on a stand 35–45 cm in front of the patient's face, screen facing the patient, at eye level.
  2. Light the face from the front. Avoid a bright window or lamp behind the patient.
  3. Turn the phone volume up and keep it plugged in.
  4. Calibrate with the patient (about 30 s). Recalibrate whenever the phone or the patient's head position changes.

Test mode: the mouse or your finger acts as the patient's gaze and holding the Space bar acts as closed eyes. NetraVani is a communication aid, not a medical device. Confirm important answers with a yes/no question.
